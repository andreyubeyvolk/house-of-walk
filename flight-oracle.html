Flight Oracle is an experimental travel divination game inspired by Jyotish, the ancient Indian system of astrology built around nine celestial bodies known as Navagraha.

Choose two seats to reveal two planetary forces, each in its original or inverted state. Their combination collapses a field of possibilities into a single reading: a travel forecast paired with a photograph from the House of Walk archive.

Part cosmic symbolism, part quantum metaphor, it turns a random choice into a moment of reflection on the journeys ahead.
